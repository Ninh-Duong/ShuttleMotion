# Basic badminton rules — English reference

Generated from rules.json. Scope: standard indoor singles and doubles. This project summary is neither an official translation nor a match adjudication engine. Consult the full source for exceptions; the latest effective version has not been confirmed.

## scoring — Scoring

A match is the best of three games. Each rally won scores one point. A game is played to 21, with a two-point lead required at 20-all and a maximum of 30. The side winning a game serves first in the next game.

**Project-authored example:** 21–20 does not end the game; 30–29 does.

Source: [BWF 5.0 (1)](https://www.badminton.no/contentassets/c0b5fd676d9a4a10bd7a14c673a7bad0/section-4.1---laws-of-badminton---26-april-2025-v5.0-1--1.pdf), clauses 7.1–7.6, pages 5.

## ends — Changing ends

Players change ends after the first game, after the second game if there is a third, and when a side first reaches 11 points in the third game. If they forget, correct this when the shuttle is out of play and keep the existing score.

**Project-authored example:** At 11–8 in the third game, the players change ends.

Source: [BWF 5.0 (1)](https://www.badminton.no/contentassets/c0b5fd676d9a4a10bd7a14c673a7bad0/section-4.1---laws-of-badminton---26-april-2025-v5.0-1--1.pdf), clauses 8.1–8.2, pages 5.

## service-position — Service and receiving positions

Server and receiver stand within diagonally opposite service courts without touching boundary lines. Some part of both feet must remain in stationary contact with the court from the start until the service is delivered.

**Project-authored example:** A served shuttle may land on the applicable line, but a server’s foot touching a service-court line is a fault.

Source: [BWF 5.0 (1)](https://www.badminton.no/contentassets/c0b5fd676d9a4a10bd7a14c673a7bad0/section-4.1---laws-of-badminton---26-april-2025-v5.0-1--1.pdf), clauses 9.1, 9.1.3–9.1.4, pages 5, 6.

## service-action — Service action

Do not unduly delay. Release the shuttle without adding spin; hit its base first while the whole shuttle is below 1.15 m at racket contact. Continue the racket’s forward movement and do not miss the shuttle. The shuttle must travel upward over the net toward the diagonal receiving court. The receiver must be ready; an attempted return shows readiness. Partners must not obscure the server or receiver.

**Project-authored example:** Serving below the height limit does not excuse adding spin when releasing the shuttle.

Source: [BWF 5.0 (1)](https://www.badminton.no/contentassets/c0b5fd676d9a4a10bd7a14c673a7bad0/section-4.1---laws-of-badminton---26-april-2025-v5.0-1--1.pdf), clauses 9.1.1–9.1.8, 9.2–9.5, pages 5, 6.

## singles — Singles service

At an even server score, serve from the right service court; at an odd score, serve from the left. If the server wins the rally, they serve again from the other court. If the receiver wins, they score and become the server.

**Project-authored example:** A server with seven points stands in the left service court; if they win, they serve from the right at eight points.

Source: [BWF 5.0 (1)](https://www.badminton.no/contentassets/c0b5fd676d9a4a10bd7a14c673a7bad0/section-4.1---laws-of-badminton---26-april-2025-v5.0-1--1.pdf), clauses 10.1–10.3, pages 6, 7.

## doubles — Doubles service and receiving order

The serving side uses its right service court at an even score and its left at an odd score; the receiver stands diagonally opposite. Players change service courts only when their side wins a point while serving. After the return of service, either partner may hit the shuttle when it is their side’s turn.

**Project-authored example:** If A1 serves and B1 initially receives, successive new servers after service changes are A1 → B2 → A2 → B1 → A1. The current server continues if their side wins a rally.

Source: [BWF 5.0 (1)](https://www.badminton.no/contentassets/c0b5fd676d9a4a10bd7a14c673a7bad0/section-4.1---laws-of-badminton---26-april-2025-v5.0-1--1.pdf), clauses 11.1–11.6, pages 7, 8.

## service-errors — Service-court and order errors

Correct service-court or serving/receiving order errors when the shuttle is out of play. Keep points already scored.

**Project-authored example:** Finding that the wrong player received after a rally does not erase earlier points.

Source: [BWF 5.0 (1)](https://www.badminton.no/contentassets/c0b5fd676d9a4a10bd7a14c673a7bad0/section-4.1---laws-of-badminton---26-april-2025-v5.0-1--1.pdf), clauses 12.1–12.2, pages 8.

## boundaries — In and out

A shuttle landing on an applicable boundary line is in. The doubles long service line is closer to the net than the back boundary used during a rally.

**Project-authored example:** A shuttle landing between the doubles long service line and the back boundary is out on doubles service but may be in during a rally.

Source: [BWF 5.0 (1)](https://www.badminton.no/contentassets/c0b5fd676d9a4a10bd7a14c673a7bad0/section-4.1---laws-of-badminton---26-april-2025-v5.0-1--1.pdf), clauses 1.3, 9.1, 13.3.1, Diagram A, pages 1, 2, 5, 8.

## faults — Common faults

Faults include illegal service; a shuttle landing out or touching a person or ceiling; catching and slinging it; successive hits by both partners; touching the net while the shuttle is in play; obstruction; or deliberate distraction.

**Project-authored example:** A racket following through over the net after contact on the striker’s side is not automatically a fault, provided it does not obstruct the opponent. One stroke touching both the frame and strings is not automatically a double hit.

Source: [BWF 5.0 (1)](https://www.badminton.no/contentassets/c0b5fd676d9a4a10bd7a14c673a7bad0/section-4.1---laws-of-badminton---26-april-2025-v5.0-1--1.pdf), clauses 13.1–13.4, pages 8, 9.

## let — Lets

A let may be called when the receiver is not ready, both sides fault during service, or another Law 14 condition applies. The rally does not count and the previous server serves again.

**Project-authored example:** After a return of service, a shuttle caught on the net is a let. If it is caught on the net during service, Law 13.2 makes it a fault instead.

Source: [BWF 5.0 (1)](https://www.badminton.no/contentassets/c0b5fd676d9a4a10bd7a14c673a7bad0/section-4.1---laws-of-badminton---26-april-2025-v5.0-1--1.pdf), clauses 14.1–14.3, pages 9, 10.

## out-of-play — Shuttle not in play

A shuttle is not in play after it hits the court, a fault or let occurs, or it hits the net or post and starts falling toward the striker’s court.

**Project-authored example:** Do not assume that every shuttle contact with the net ends the rally.

Source: [BWF 5.0 (1)](https://www.badminton.no/contentassets/c0b5fd676d9a4a10bd7a14c673a7bad0/section-4.1---laws-of-badminton---26-april-2025-v5.0-1--1.pdf), clauses 15.1–15.3, pages 10.
